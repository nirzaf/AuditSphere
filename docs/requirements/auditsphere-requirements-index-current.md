## Decision boundary

The [ADR register](../architecture/adr/auditsphere-architecture-index-adr-register-current.md) records approved technical and delegated product decisions within their explicit scope. Open methodology, accounting treatment, legal or commercial policy, partner/client assent, live provider behavior, and release acceptance remain with their qualified owners. Do not infer them from a proposed specification or a passing test.

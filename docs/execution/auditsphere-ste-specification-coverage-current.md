## Automatic invoice drafts and preserved finance approval

The specification authorizes automatic 50/50 invoice generation. The local worker may generate **drafts** only under an explicitly configured standing policy naming a current firm-wide FinanceManager and approving Administrator. The policy is disabled by default. Queued requests bind both identities and session epochs, the exact fee agreement and milestone; withdrawal of either authority blocks execution. Engagement-letter issuance creates the reviewed fee agreement/milestones in the same local transaction. The advance draft becomes eligible after that gated letter exists; the balance draft becomes eligible after advance payment and financial-package release. FinanceReviewer approval and FinanceManager posting remain the existing independent commands; automation does not approve, post, send invoices or execute payment. Every draft uses the existing source allocation and durable operation evidence. Deployment must configure this policy explicitly rather than impersonating a Partner as finance staff.

## Deliverable assembly and portal safety

The client portal requires completion of its Microsoft identity-path first-sign-in flow before signed-LOR or PBC uploads. A signed-LOR upload is a readable, unencrypted PDF of at most 10 MB, tied to the exact acknowledged generated version and a named management signatory. The Engagement Partner records signature, authority and completeness verification; later changes to underlying audit facts make both the generated letter and its scan stale. New scans, verifications, seals and bundles are append-only. Scopes and current session epochs are rechecked for downloads, verification, signing and assembly.

Bundle assembly is bounded to 100 MB and bounded correspondence counts. It uses stored document bytes, exact released financial PDF evidence, current package reviews and a posted balance invoice with a paid advance. The Partner confirms the exact financial statements and the certification cover retains the report's original signature/seal versions. Hashes cover the exact manifest text and ZIP bytes. Client PBC writes and representation uploads share the client release guard so a final release cannot overtake an upload transaction. No access token, private key, administrator password or reusable consent callback is included in bundle evidence.

Apply the new migration through the normal approved deployment process. Configure `AutomaticFeeInvoices:Enabled`, `FinanceUserId` and `ApprovingAdministratorId` privately on the general worker if standing draft authorization is desired; both identities must hold current firm-wide grants. The policy remains off by default. Migrations were exercised in isolated PostgreSQL test schemas; that does not apply them to Development or production. No Wiki publication or live tenant change is implied by these repository changes.

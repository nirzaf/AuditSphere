Workflow reference for ISA / IFRS-related audit evidence

Reference only STE v2.1 requirements
Implementation crosswalk: This is an illustrative workflow reference, not the architecture or acceptance authority. Check current architecture, current requirements, and execution evidence before treating any capability as available. PRE

Preparer (Associate)

Prepares scoped procedures, evidence references, and firm time entries.

REV

Reviewer (Manager)

Reviews assigned work, resolves rework, and prepares planning evidence.

APP

Approver (Partner)

Records risk decisions, reviews required areas, signs reports, and authorizes gated actions.

CLI

Client Portal (PBC)

Client access is scoped; portal invitations and provider access have separate gates.

Flow 1: Commercial Acceptance and Engagement Activation

Two independent decisions and a Partner activation review govern progression; neither decision is a cryptographic key.

ISA 210 & ISA 220
flowchart TD Lead["Lead and client profile"] --> Proposal["Prepare and dispatch current proposal"] Proposal --> ClientAcceptance["Client accepts current commercial terms"] Proposal --> PartnerRisk["Partner records unconditional risk decision"] ClientAcceptance --> Gate{"Both current decisions are valid"} PartnerRisk --> Gate Gate -->|No| Blocked["Engagement remains blocked"] Gate -->|Yes| Draft["Create draft engagement"] Draft --> Letter["Generate route-specific engagement letter"] Letter --> Fee["Prepare fee agreement and advance invoice when linked"] Fee --> Activation["Partner reviews and activates engagement"] Activation --> Advance{"Linked advance milestone paid"} Advance -->|No linked agreement or paid| Active["Engagement activated after other gates clear"] Advance -->|Unpaid| Blocked Active --> Portal["Portal intent becomes eligible for invitation"]

Independent acceptance checks

  • Client commercial acceptance must match the current proposal and quotation revision.
  • A Partner records an unconditional risk acceptance for the client and service route.
  • These are human decisions; this diagram does not claim that external KYC or AML checks are automated.

Client portal access boundary

  • Client portal commands require the signed-in client identity and explicit client or engagement scope.
  • Invitation and Microsoft provisioning are separate from local engagement activation.
  • Production tenant consent and provider acceptance must be verified independently.

Flow 3: Split Dashboard, Fieldwork & Three-Tier Review Matrix

Revision-bound work, scoped evidence, review and rework, plus the current completion gate.

Revision-bound work
Writes carry the expected revision and current scope. This is not a promise of lock-free concurrent edits.
Evidence references
Scoped procedures can reference digital evidence and physical record identifiers; archive controls are handled separately.
Critical confirmation blockers
A missing critical response blocks report issuance. A holding-letter dispatch is tied to the current blocker set and configured completion contact route.

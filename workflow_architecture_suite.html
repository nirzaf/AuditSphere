Flow 1 of 5 · Commercial

Lead to Engagement Activation

Dual-Key clearance is mandatory before generating the Engagement Letter. A recorded 50% advance payment is mandatory before entering portal-active planning; no optional linked-fee bypass.

ISA 210 & ISA 220

Commercial and Risk Records

  • Key 1: recorded client commercial acceptance. Key 2: Partner digitally signed new-client acceptance or recurring-client continuance checklist. Both are mandatory for the Engagement Letter.
  • New clients require UBO, AML/KYC, integrity, viability, independence and conflict checks. Recurring clients require prior fees, management/shareholding changes, borrowings, litigation, and fraud/regulatory-change reviews. Automated third-party screening is not mandated.

Activation and Client Access

  • Use ISA 210 templates for statutory audit or agreed-upon procedures; include service period, fee and deadlines. Generate and digitally sign the letter after Dual-Key clearance.
  • 50% advance payment and reference must be recorded before activation. Auto-generate the receipt; provision client workspace, initiate Microsoft Entra invitation, and establish PBC status tracking (per approved ADR-0003, Microsoft sign-in replaces emailed temporary credentials and password reset).
  • BLOCKED_EXTERNAL — live portal provisioning and the first-sign-in evidence gate need a live Microsoft tenant. Per ADR-0003 the portal uses Microsoft sign-in rather than emailed temporary passwords, so the equivalent first-sign-in evidence must be observed; local orchestration records the intent and reconciles the result and does not prove sign-in.

Mandatory state guards

No Engagement Letter without C-01. No PORTAL_ACTIVE_PLANNING without C-02. Client portal upload statuses: Pending Upload, Under Review, Approved, Rejected/Re-upload Required; each rejection needs a reason.

Flow 2 of 5 · Planning

Trial Balance Materiality and Risk Planning

After mandatory new/recurring-client checks, provision five folders, schedule staff, import and map Excel/CSV TB, compute materiality, and require Partner planning approval before fieldwork.

ISA 320 Standard

GREEN · Low Risk

Balance < TE and no overriding high inherent risk

Lower band: staffing rank rules apply; procedures and professional conclusions remain human-led.

AMBER · Moderate Risk

TE < Balance < PM; exact TE/PM equality requires approved policy

Senior substantive testing and sampling; high inherent risk overrides the balance band. Equality at exactly TE or PM remains a decision, not an approved rule.

RED · Critical / High Risk

Balance > PM or high inherent risk / estimates

Red band: manager-level execution and Partner review rules apply, including significant or fraud risks.
Materiality Metric Formula Reference ISA 320 Execution Framework
Planning Materiality (PM) Planning threshold used with qualitative considerations
PM = Benchmark base × selected approved %
Tolerable Error (TE) / Performance Materiality Tolerable error is derived from PM using the selected policy percentage
TE = 50% to 75% of PM
Summary of Audit Differences (SAD) Threshold Configured SAD threshold used by the differences workflow
SAD = 3% to 5% of PM
Practical Rounding Guard Manager or Senior Manager records rationale; a new effective draft still needs Partner approval
Each adjusted threshold must remain within ±5% of its computed value

Specified benchmark limits and planning inputs

PBT 5–10% (per approved ADR-0011, Profit Before Tax is applied without normalization, superseding the initial v2.1 baseline description); Revenue 0.5–2%; Total Assets 0.5–1%; Equity/Net Assets 1–2%. Required five-fold taxonomy: 01_Administration & Planning, 02_Trial Balance & Schedules, 03_Fieldwork & Testing, 04_Drafts & Deliverables, 05_Final Signed Archive. Equality at TE or PM is unresolved: fail closed pending approved classification policy.

Flow 3 of 5 · Execution

Fieldwork, Review and Completion Gates

The split P/L and Balance Sheet FSLI dashboard launches analytical review and workprograms. Preparers submit all assigned tests; mandatory rework and critical-confirmation gates precede Partner clearance.

Fieldwork & ISA 505 / 570

Revision-Checked Work

Separate FSLIs support parallel auditors without overwrite. Revision checks are a proposed technical protection, not a replacement for required row-level concurrency.

Evidence and Revision Controls

Preserve direct digital links to PDF, spreadsheet and PBC evidence plus a dedicated physical file-index field such as X-1 / Box 3 / Shelf B.

Holding Letter Dispatch Status

Critical unreturned confirmations must block report release and automatically generate a Holding Letter. Delivery tracking may record queued/sent status without falsely asserting receipt.

Testing and review acceptance criteria

Per FSLI: Current Year, Prior Year, % variance, AR Test, Audit Workprogram; assertion steps, editable ad-hoc steps; Monetary Unit Sampling, systematic random, stratified attribute sampling; ISA 570 check. Reviewer returns rejected work with compulsory comments; SRM auto-compiles on Manager approval.

Flow 4 of 5 · Reporting

Partner Opinion, Five-Part Release and Permanent Archive

Partner-only opinion, modified-opinion rationale, signature and seal, all five deliverables, final 50% invoice and client upload freeze precede a 60-day or Partner-triggered irreversible archive lock.

ISA 700, 705 & ISA 230
  1. Part 1

    Report and Statements

    Digitally signed and sealed Independent Auditor Report with certified Financial Statements. Signed using firm-held X.509 certificate-based PDF signing per approved ADR-0016 (production certificate deployment and legal acceptance remain open external gates).
  2. Part 2

    Mgmt Letter

    Management Letter with deficiency, impact and auditor recommendation for each finding.
  3. Part 3

    Representation

    Client-letterhead Letter of Representation template for executive signing and re-upload.
  4. Part 4

    Correspondences

    Management correspondence, formal audit inquiries, confirmation results and cleared-query trail; JSON is an optional implementation format.
  5. Part 5

    50% Balance Note

    Automatically generate the final fee note / invoice for the remaining 50% when the bundle is released.

Irreversible archive semantics

C-12 requires read-only conversion at 60 calendar days from Partner signature OR earlier manual Partner lock, with deletions, updates and overwrites permanently prohibited and an immutable timestamped audit log. Per approved ADR-0009, the platform's independent release/archive store is authoritative for sealed artifacts, while SharePoint serves as an auxiliary read-only client copy. Post-lock amendment handling and cryptographic signing comply with ADR-0016; any future approved correction must leave the locked original untouched, never transition the original archive backwards.

BLOCKED_EXTERNAL — provider-enforced immutability (SharePoint retention and the permanent deletion/overwrite prohibition) is not established by a local freeze and requires live tenant evidence.

Specified Profitability and Actual-Cost Analytics

Source-defined engagement profitability = Contracted engagement fee − Σ logged hours × role charge-out rate. This is a charge-out-based indicator, not audited net profit. The implemented practice analytics additionally derive actual cost from a separately recorded staff cost rate (never the charge-out rate), with profit, margin, realization and billed/collected taken from posted invoice lines and allocated receipts; a measure missing its inputs is reported as unavailable rather than zero.

Fee milestones: Mandatory 50% advance and remaining 50% final-fee note in the baseline.

Internal books remain a dedicated module

Record rent/facilities, salaries/benefits, Partner withdrawals and petty cash. Required outputs: firm Trial Balance, monthly Profit & Loss statement and client Accounts Receivable aging. The audited client TB is never posted into the firm books merely because a source TB is imported.

Flow 6 · Record-to-Report Modules 20–25

Client Accounting, TB/GL Intake & Financial Statements

Governs per-client trial balance and general ledger intake, chart of accounts versioning, FSLI taxonomy mapping, adjusting journal entries (AJE), lead schedules, and multi-format financial statement generation (Balance Sheet, P&L, Cash Flow, Changes in Equity, Disclosures). Strictly segregated from the firm practice ledger.

numeric(19,6) Scale Maker-Checker AJE Multi-Format Export

Precision & Rounding

All monetary amounts are stored as numeric(19,6) with MidpointRounding.ToEven per ADR-0014. Control totals enforce exact balance: sum(Debits) == sum(Credits).

Maker-Checker AJE

Adjusting journal entries require strict separation of duties. Preparers record proposed adjustments with supporting workpapers; Audit Senior or Partner must approve before posting.

Financial Boundaries

Client accounting records exist in an isolated workspace. Client adjustments never mutate the audit firm's practice ledger or consolidation run records.

Flow 7 · Record-to-Report Module 26

Multi-Currency Group Consolidation

Supports corporate groups with multiple legal entities, versioned consolidation perimeters, intercompany transaction matching, automated elimination journals, and IAS 21 currency translation. Strictly preserves component company ledger immutability.

Non-Mutation Invariant IAS 21 CTA Intercompany Matching

Non-Mutation Invariant

Consolidation eliminations and translation entries exist exclusively within the consolidation run. Consolidation never modifies, posts to, or overwrites component entity books.

IAS 21 Currency Translation

Assets and liabilities translate at the closing rate; income and expense accounts translate at transaction or average rates. Net exchange differences accumulate in equity as CTA.

Intercompany Matching

Pairwise reconciliation identifies trade, loan, and management fee differences. Variances exceeding materiality threshold trigger discrepancy schedules for audit verification.

Flow 8 · Security, Storage & Architecture

Microsoft 365, Least Privilege & Authoritative Storage

Implements server-side Microsoft Entra OIDC authentication, scoped Graph application roles without global administrator rights, dedicated client SharePoint sites, and an independent authoritative release store with cryptographic SHA-256 integrity.

No Entra Admin Roles Sites.Selected Scoped SHA-256 Authoritative Store

Least Privilege Policy

AuditSphere RoleGrant is the sole source of application authorization. No role maps to Entra directory admin roles; every Graph capability identity holds exactly one application permission.

Authoritative Release Store

Per ADR-0009, issued audit deliverables are authoritatively stored in the platform release store with SHA-256 hash seals. SharePoint provides a secondary client-accessible copy.

Durable Background Workers

External Graph and SharePoint interactions run through durable workers with idempotent retries. Uncertain external outcomes fail closed without corrupting audit records.

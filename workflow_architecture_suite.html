Application Architecture and Financial Boundaries

AuditSphere is one ASP.NET Core modular monolith with Angular presentation, API and worker hosts. This page is an implementation reference; current code and authority documents govern behavior.

Strict Data Isolation Guarded Workflows

One Modular Monolith

Domain, Application, Infrastructure, API, Angular UI, legacy rollback host and Worker are parts of one application architecture.

Separate Financial Boundaries

Firm books, client accounting and group consolidation remain separate; consolidation does not mutate component books.

Flow 1 of 5 · Commercial

Lead to Engagement Activation

Client commercial acceptance and current Partner risk approval are separate records. Activation follows the configured commercial and payment gates.

ISA 210 & ISA 220

Commercial and Risk Records

  • Client acceptance and Partner risk approval are separately recorded and must be current for the same service route.
  • The platform records decisions; this diagram does not claim automated AML, UBO or PEP screening.

Activation and Client Access

  • Engagement letters use a configured template for the selected service route.
  • When a fee agreement is linked, its advance must be paid and allocated before activation. Portal access remains identity- and scope-controlled.

Flow 2 of 5 · Planning

Trial Balance Materiality and Risk Planning

The engine derives thresholds from mapped trial-balance inputs. Approval binds to the current assessment; practical rounding creates a new effective draft.

ISA 320 Standard

GREEN · Low Risk

Balance < TE

Lower band: staffing rank rules apply; procedures and professional conclusions remain human-led.

AMBER · Moderate Risk

TE ≤ Balance ≤ PM

Amber band: senior execution and manager review rules apply to assigned work.

RED · Critical / High Risk

Balance > PM or high inherent risk / estimates

Red band: manager-level execution and Partner review rules apply, including significant or fraud risks.
Materiality Metric Formula Reference ISA 320 Execution Framework
Planning Materiality (PM) Planning threshold used with qualitative considerations
Base × % (e.g. 5% PBT or 1% Revenue)
Tolerable Error (TE) / Performance Materiality Tolerable error is derived from PM using the selected policy percentage
50% to 75% of PM under the current policy range
Summary of Audit Differences (SAD) Threshold Configured SAD threshold used by the differences workflow
3% to 5% of PM
Practical Rounding Guard Manager or Senior Manager records rationale; a new effective draft still needs Partner approval
Each adjusted threshold must remain within ±5% of its computed value

Flow 3 of 5 · Execution

Fieldwork, Review and Completion Gates

Assigned procedures move through revision-checked preparation and review. Current completion evidence and critical confirmations govern Partner clearance.

Fieldwork & ISA 505 / 570

Revision-Checked Work

Revision and scope checks protect writes. Concurrent edits can be rejected when a user submits stale work.

Evidence and Revision Controls

Workpaper results use current revision and scope checks. Physical record handling remains governed by the firm's archive procedures.

Holding Letter Dispatch Status

Critical confirmation blockers keep the completion gate closed. A current holding letter can be queued through configured contact routing; queued delivery is not proof of receipt.

Flow 4 of 5 · Reporting

Partner Opinion, Deliverable Bundle and Local File Freeze

Partner decisions and exact current evidence feed local bundle assembly. Report signature schedules the local freeze; provider-level immutability is separate.

ISA 700, 705 & ISA 230
  1. Part 1

    Report and Statements

    Signed auditor report and certified statements from the exact released financial package. Signature and seal are approved images, not certificate-backed e-signatures.
  2. Part 2

    Mgmt Letter

    Current management letter included in the assembled bundle.
  3. Part 3

    Representation

    Generated representation letter plus the exact signed management scan.
  4. Part 4

    Correspondences

    Management correspondence and review trail in a bounded JSON audit-trail file.
  5. Part 5

    50% Balance Note

    Fee note references the posted balance invoice; finance approval, payment and delivery remain separate.

Flow 5 of 5 · Firm Operations

Practice Time, Charge-Out Rates and Firm Books

Approved versioned charge-out cards feed time and budget views. A charge-out rate is not payroll or staff cost; practice books remain separate from client accounting.

How to Read Charge-Out Rates

STE QAR charge-out rates are pricing inputs for time and budget views. Do not interpret them as payroll cost or realized profit.

Fee milestones: Default 50% advance; invoice posting and payment follow the finance workflow.

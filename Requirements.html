What the system must achieve

Support unlimited client entities, historical engagements and working papers without a per-file subscription penalty.

Connect every engagement stage

Commercial pipeline, acceptance, governance, fieldwork, review, client deliverables and practice-management workflows are connected by explicit gates.

Standardize professional procedures

03 · Responsibility matrix · source §1.2

Roles and decision ownership

These four personas and their responsibilities are directly taken from the original responsibility matrix. A client cannot perform internal audit approvals.

PREPARER

Audit Associate / Junior Auditor

  • Executes assigned financial statement line item (FSLI) audit test procedures.
  • Uploads digital working papers and inputs physical binder index codes (X-1, Box 3).
  • Submits completed testing packages for managerial review.
  • Logs daily operational hours against assigned engagement tasks.
REVIEWER

Audit Senior / Audit Manager

  • Verifies substantive testing and recalculated schedules.
  • Issues inline review notes and initiates the rework loop for incomplete tests.
  • Determines sampling parameters and calculates engagement materiality.
  • Prepares the Summary Review Memorandum (SRM) for the partner.
  • Tracks engagement budgets, team hours, and delivery milestones.
APPROVER

Engagement Partner

  • Evaluates and signs off on the Dual-Key Acceptance Gate (AML/KYC).
  • Authorizes commercial proposals and executes Engagement Letters.
  • Clears high-risk (Red) audit areas and formally signs off on the SRM.
  • Selects the final Audit Opinion, applies digital signatures and firm seals.
  • Authorizes final deliverable bundles and enforces regulatory file locks.
CLIENT

Client Coordinator / CFO / MD

  • Accesses an isolated, tokenized external workspace (PBC Portal).
  • Views requested audit documentation with real-time review status badges.
  • Uploads requested financial schedules, trial balances, and voucher evidence.
  • Receives invoices, receipts, holding letters, and final deliverables.
  • Access freezes automatically upon engagement sign-off.

05 · Process and decision diagrams · source §3–4

Business workflows with mandatory gates

The first five diagrams correspond to the Word document's five end-to-end flows. A sixth diagram expands the PBC portal's compulsory upload-review loop.

Flow 01 · SOURCE §3.1, §4.1, §4.2

Lead → acceptance → advance → portal

Source-aligned diagram

Two independent approval keys are required. A proposal or an accepted quote does not by itself release the Engagement Letter. Payment must be recorded before the next lifecycle state.

Flow 02 · SOURCE §3.2, §4.2

Risk acceptance → planning → materiality

Source-aligned diagram

The new-client and continuance checks differ. The statutory calendar, folders, TB import, threshold calculations and formal Partner sign-off belong to the planning stage.

Flow 03 · SOURCE §3.3, §4.3

FSLI fieldwork → reviewer rework → partner

Source-aligned diagram

Auditors can work on separate financial statement line items concurrently. Return with Comments requires a reason and generates an Under Rework loop. Critical confirmation failures block reporting.

Flow 04 · SOURCE §3.4, §4.4

Final opinion → bundle → lock

Source-aligned diagram

A modified opinion requires an affected FSLI and written basis. The 60-day archive clock starts from the signature date, not from client delivery.

Flow 05 · SOURCE §3.5, §4.5

Time logging → realization → practice books

Source-aligned diagram

Practice time capture runs in parallel with engagements. The firm’s own ledger is separate from the client’s audit TB.

07 · Governance · scope fidelity

What is specified — and what is not

Mandatory baseline

  • Five core modules and eleven exact lifecycle states.
  • Four original roles and their responsibilities.
  • Dual-Key client acceptance and 50/50 billing arrangement.
  • TB-driven materiality, risk-coded FSLI fieldwork and review.
  • Five-part deliverables, portal freeze and 60-day archive lock.
  • Time charging, profitability and the firm's internal practice ledger.

Previously proposed · not baseline

  • Full-scale client general-ledger and journal posting.
  • Client accounting reconciliations and complete close management.
  • Group consolidation and foreign-currency translation.
  • Intercompany eliminations and consolidated reporting.
  • Financial-period reopening and formal restatement.
  • Other extended accounting services not expressly included in source §4.

These ideas appeared in or were discussed by the previous HTML presentation, but are not specified as full modules in the attached Word baseline. The previous HTML separately described client payroll, inventory operations and bank-payment initiation as outside its scope; that exclusion was not expressly decided in Word v2.1. Add or exclude through an approved scope decision.

Open specification decisions — do not silently invent rules

The source does not fully define tie-break behaviour at exactly TE or PM, how automatic statuses are retried after integration failures, the signed-PDF technology/provider, operational retention after a read-only lock, full sampling calculation parameters, detailed authentication/token-expiry policy, or the handling of cancelled/reopened engagements. These need authorized decisions; they are not made mandatory by this document.
